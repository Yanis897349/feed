# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project.
Contributors should communicate constructively, assume good intent, respect
different experiences, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards. To
report conduct concerns, contact a maintainer privately. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project.
